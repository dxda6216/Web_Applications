Channel information (optional)

Choose the TRACES or TD file first.
Enter details and temperatures (°C)

The details (e.g. sample, genotype, treatment) and the temperature of each channel are added to the graph titles and the Excel files. Temperature in °C with 2 decimal places, 0–60 °C (arrows / Up, Down keys: ±0.1 °C, Shift ±1 °C). Enter them here or import a Text, CSV or Excel file with the columns Channel, Details, Temperature (°C) – a header row is optional; without a channel column the rows are channels 00, 01, … in order. Details are usually in English; Chinese, Japanese and Korean also work (for the graphs, a font with those characters is downloaded when needed). Files: UTF-8 is best; CSV files saved by Japanese, Chinese or Korean Excel are recognised too (most reliably with a header row).

Help
          
Middle plot: Move – drag a peak/trough to its new time (it snaps to the
          nearest measured time point); Remove – click a marker, or drag a box to remove all markers inside it; Add peak / Add trough –
          click at the time. Dragging elsewhere scrolls in time (in Remove mode it draws the box; Shift+wheel scrolls), the mouse wheel zooms, a double-click
          shows the whole recording, a click in the top (raw-data) plot moves the view there.
          Left/Right arrow keys switch channels.

          
Actogram: click a point to use / not use it for the period–phase regression
          (ringed points are used); drag a box to select the points inside (Shift+drag: deselect).
          With Regression points: Remove, a click or a box takes the points out of the
          regression instead (Shift+drag then selects).
          Regression range: only the peaks/troughs in this time range are used (to 0 = end,
          both empty = all); shown as purple dashed lines.

          
Peaks/troughs from: pick them from the centred moving average (1 = no
          smoothing, 3, 5, 7, 9 or 11 points; default 9) of the detrended data (default) or of the raw
          data, in every channel (the same options as on the Basic tab; the peaks are detected
          again).

          
Detrend and sine fit are set per channel; changing the detrending detects
          the peaks/troughs of that channel again. Spectra data range: the time range of the
          data analysed by the wavelet, FFT, periodograms and MEM (all channels), drawn as coloured
          bars just above the top plot. The spectra of the channel shown are drawn under the
          actogram (wavelet: red = ridge, white = cone of influence, grey = not analysed; FFT,
          MEM: shaded = period range; periodograms scaled to their maximum, dotted =
          significance level) and their results are listed at the bottom right.
          The settings on the left (tabs) apply to the next “Analyse and review”.
